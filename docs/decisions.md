## D-037 — Imported voiceover: upload or record, rights note, lip-sync, no mature lip-sync with an uploaded voice (2026-11-27)

Owner: users bring their own voiceover; TTS and voice cloning stay “coming soon” (no provider chosen).

- **Import.** Voiceover audio (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG; or a browser recording through MediaRecorder, WebM/Opus) goes into Assets as an audio asset through the shared upload queue (D-011, straight to R2); media.process probes its length and stores the waveform. It lands on the voice lane at the playhead (Effects → Import voiceover). On export every voice-lane clip is evened out to −16 LUFS (one-pass `loudnorm`) before the mix; social presets then normalise the mix to −14 LUFS (D-034).
- **Rights.** The uploader confirms “I have the rights to use this voice” (one line, on import and on the Avatar page). It is stored on the asset (`meta.voiceRights {by, at}`; `voiceRights: true` on `POST /api/assets/uploads`, or `POST /api/assets/:id/voice-rights`). Lip-sync from the editor refuses a voice without it (`VOICE_RIGHTS_REQUIRED`).
- **Real-person rule (D-005).** An uploaded voice may be a real, identifiable person's, so a **mature lip-sync with an uploaded voice is blocked** (plan code `mature_voice`, `packages/core/generation/specPlan.ts`). Until a TTS provider exists, that means no mature lip-sync at all. Non-mature lip-sync is allowed.
- **Editor.** Drag, trim, gain and fade work as on any clip; Effects → “Duck under the voiceover” sets `duckUnder` on a music clip (new `setDuck` op; the renderer's sidechain compressor does the ducking). “Split voiceover on pauses” runs ffmpeg `silencedetect` in the container (`/silences`) and cuts in the middle of each pause (parts ≥ 0.8 s), then moves part *i* to the start of shot *i* when that keeps the lane free of overlaps.
- **Lip-sync.** Effects → “Lip-sync to voice” on a shot: the shot's first frame (or a chosen picture) and the voice clip's range (cut in the container, `/audio-cut`) go to the Avatar lip-sync model (`lumina/wan2.7-i2v-spicy`, D-009) as an `avatar` spec through quote → hold → settle (RunConfirm above the threshold). The result replaces the shot in place. The Avatar tool page takes the same uploaded voiceover.
- **Agent.** When a project's voice lane holds an imported voiceover, the agent's context lists each clip's place, length and transcript (Whisper, D-033; the transcript runs free on import) so plans can pace shots to the voice.

## D-036 — Extend clip: last frame → image-to-video, placed after the original (2026-11-27)

- The container extracts the clip's true last frame (`-sseof` and decode to the end with `-update 1`, `/last-frame`), stored as an image asset (`meta.frameOf: "last"`, a platform asset when the clip was generated, D-005).
- The continuation is a normal `video` PromptSpec with `inputs.firstFrameAssetId` = that frame and the user's prompt: quote → hold → settle, RunConfirm and the paywall like every run. Model: the clip's own model when it takes a first frame (`i2v`), else Auto. Ratio follows the clip; Enhance prompt is off (the continuation keeps the shot's look).
- Placement (`timeline_placements`, migration 0031): when the run lands the new clip goes right after the original on its track; later clips move right by its length. The join skips the new clip's first frame (it repeats the original's last frame), so the cut is seamless. A run started through `POST …/placed-runs` shows a planned (not generated) clip at once; a failed or canceled run takes it back out and closes the gap.
- Hyperouter lists an `extend` operation on three models (Grok Imagine Video, Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.5 Flash); we use first-frame i2v instead because it works on every i2v model and keeps the original untouched.

## D-034 — Export presets, reframing, Smoother motion and Resize (2026-11-27)

- **Presets:** `vertical_9x16` (1080×1920, TikTok / Reels / Shorts, 8 Mbps, VBV 10 Mbps), `square_1x1` (1080×1080, 6 Mbps), `landscape_16x9` (1920×1080, YouTube, 10 Mbps, AAC 384 kbps) next to the original-aspect `mp4_1080p` / `mp4_720p`. All H.264 High, yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz; social presets normalise the mix to **−14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP** (one-pass `loudnorm`). Several presets export at once (one export per preset, `batch_id`). Values live in `packages/core/src/export.ts` (`PRESET_ENCODE`).
- **Smart reframe:** when the preset's aspect differs from the cut's, each shot of the base video track covers the frame and is cropped around its subject instead of letterboxed. The subject path comes from saliency on 64×64 grayscale frames sampled at 3 fps (edge energy + motion, center prior), smoothed both ways, dead-zoned and speed-limited (≤ 0.25 frame widths per second). We do not call the paid `lumina/face-detect` model per frame: the free tracker runs next to the render. Manual override per clip (`reframe`: auto / center / a fixed point).
- **Smoother motion:** frame interpolation to 48 or 60 fps with ffmpeg `minterpolate` (motion-compensated, scene cuts not blended), labelled honestly (“adds in-between frames by estimating motion; fast moves can wobble; slower to export”), with a 4 s low-resolution preview at the playhead (not saved to Assets). Exports with it are capped at 3 minutes.
- **Resize, not AI upscaling.** Neither the fixture nor the live Hyperouter listing (2026-11-27) has an upscale or super-resolution model, so “Resize to 1080p / 4K” is Lanczos scaling plus a mild unsharp in the container, and says so. Plan caps apply (D-006): 1080p video needs Plus, 4K needs Pro; images up to 1080p on every plan, 4K on Pro. The result is a new asset and, from a canvas node, its new version.
- Container work (exports, previews, reframing, interpolation, resize) is free like exports.

## D-033 — Auto captions with Workers AI Whisper (2026-11-27)

- Model: `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` (the newest Whisper on Workers AI: word timings, language detection), through an `AI` binding in the staging and production Worker envs. The container renders the audio to transcribe (whole mix, the voice lane, or one clip) as mono 16 kHz MP3 chunks of 4 minutes; each chunk is one Whisper call in PostWorkflow.

Captions are free with caps instead (15 minutes per job, 240 minutes per workspace per day; `POST_PRODUCTION` in `packages/core/src/pricing/config.ts`). Members only (D-007).

## D-024 — Result review: a cheap vision model reviews every output like a director (2026-11-27)

Owner: “the agent should review generated results the way a director would, not just plan.”

- **What runs:** after `store` and before `commit`, the Generate workflow's `review-<attempt>` step sends each output to the cheapest vision-capable chat model on the gateway (the safety vision list, `SAFETY_VISION_MODELS`: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash first, D-002): the image itself, or two frames of a clip (¼ and ¾ through, from the FfmpegRenderer; the poster when the container is down). Up to 4 takes per job in one request, plus up to 2 declared reference pictures (character, product, first frame). The brief carries the prompt as the user sees it, the rendered rewrite (D-020), the ratio, the length, the takes asked for, the characters' descriptors and the shot's required text. `packages/core/src/review`, `apps/api/src/runs/review.ts`.
- **Verdict:** per take, a score 1–5 (or null when it does not apply) for `anatomy` (hands, faces, limbs), `text` (garbled or unwanted text and logos), `adherence` (subject, action, setting, count), `continuity` (declared descriptors and reference picture; null without either), `framing` (plus a deterministic check of the pixel size against the ratio) and `artifacts` (melting, duplication, frames that disagree); issues (`dimension`, `minor|major`, a 12-word note); a fix (the whole revised prompt, extra negative terms, or a fresh seed, with a one-line note). Overall = ½ worst + ½ mean, a major issue caps it at 2.5; good ≥ 4, fair ≥ 3, poor below. A take needs a retry at a dimension ≤ 2 or a major issue.
- **Stored:** `jobs.review` (JSON) and `jobs.review_cost_usd`, each asset's `meta.review`, the node version's `output.review`; ProjectRoom exposes the summary as `version_quality` (snapshot), `quality` (`node.version`) and `reviews` (`getGraph`). Migration `0032_review`.
- **Takes:** a multi-take job (count > 1) ranks its takes (score, then fewer major issues, then the worst dimension, then output order) and the best one becomes the version's asset; the others stay in `output.assetIds`.
- **Acting on it:** the canvas node shows a small quality badge on the result; the result viewer (canvas, Activity) shows the badge, the issues and the fix. Read and Edit mode, and runs the user started: “Retry with fix” writes the fix to the node (`fixParams`: prompt, negative terms appended, a fresh seed) and runs it through the normal quote and confirm; the user runs it. **Auto mode (D-021):** a run started with `serverOptions.auto = { budgetLeft, turnId?, turnBudget? }` retries automatically **at most once per node** per turn (`review_retries`, primary key budget key + node) when the retry fits what is left of the turn's budget; the retry is a new run (`retry_of`, `options.reviewRetryOf`) with its own quote, hold and version, and never retries again. After it, the better version stays current (ties keep the retry); keeping the original puts back the params the fix changed. Over budget → the offer instead. `run.start` (D-021) passes the turn's id and budget in `startRun(…, serverOptions)` (what is left is recomputed at retry time from the runs started in that turn and the retries already made); request bodies cannot set it.
- **Never blocks:** a review that fails (gateway, timeout, invalid answer, no pictures, preference off) leaves the output unreviewed; the job commits as before. The review is not a safety check (D-005 stays in `packages/core/safety`).
- **Money: covered from the margin, not quoted or held.** A review costs about $0.002 per output on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (`QUALITY_REVIEW.expectedUsdPerOutput`, ≈ 3,000–5,000 input tokens with the pictures and ≈ 450 output tokens per take) and is refused above `maxUsdPerOutput` ($0.005) at the review model's prices (`reviewMaxUsd`). Every output is at least 1 credit and earns at least `USD_PER_CREDIT × (1 − 1/MARKUP)` ≈ $0.0067 over the gateway cost, so the review fits inside the cheapest margin; quoting it would add a credit to cheap runs and change every client-side estimate for a fraction of a cent. The cost the gateway reports is recorded on the job (`review_cost_usd`) for accounting; nothing is held, settled or charged for it.
- **Preference:** Settings → Preferences → “Review results” (`prefs.reviewResults`, `user_prefs.review_results`), **on** by default; off skips the review (no call, no badge).
- **Agent awareness:** the canvas and editor context lists the current versions whose review found a problem, worst first (`reviewLines`); the system prompt (“Result reviews”) has the agent say the worst one once, briefly, and offer the retry (“Shot 3's hands are off. Retry with the fix?”), with no narration of the check. Evals: `packages/agent/evals/review.ts` grades the verdict-to-action logic offline (action, best take, kept version, fix params, agent line).

## D-023 — UI localization: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean (2026-11-27)

Owner: Asian markets matter. The UI ships in English (source), `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `ja` and `ko`. This supersedes the language list in `handoff.md` §6 (first wave ja, zh-TW, zh-HK, ko): `zh-HK` is no longer listed and reads as Traditional Chinese.

- **Files:** `messages/<locale>/<feature>.json`, the same keys as `messages/en/<feature>.json`. English ships in the main bundle; each other locale is one lazy chunk (`apps/web/src/i18n/locales/<locale>.ts`), so the English bundle does not grow. Missing or empty keys fall back to English per key. `email.json` holds the Worker's email strings and is not in the SPA bundle.

A saved choice is final (a saved Deutsch is English text with German formats, never the browser's Korean). - **Emails** (sign-in code, email change, account deletion, invites, data export) follow the recipient's saved language (by user id, else the address of an existing account), else the request's `Accept-Language` for an address without an account (sign-in and email-change codes), else English; invites to people without an account are English. Internal feedback mail stays English.
- **Legal pages stay English** (`lang="en"` on the document), with a note in the UI language: "This document is in English. The English version governs." (`legal.englishGoverns`). The prerendered static pages (read without JavaScript) carry one note listing it in every translated language.

## D-030 — Resilience: gateway circuit breaker, gateway seam, nightly Durable Object backups (2026-11-27)

Owner request (operations). We depend on one gateway (D-002) and on Durable Object state for money and projects.

- **Circuit breaker** (`packages/core/src/ops/breaker.ts`, state in D1 `gateway_breaker`, `apps/api/src/ops/breaker.ts`): 5 outage signals (HTTP 5xx, 408/timeouts, network errors; never 4xx such as content rejections) within 60 s open it for 60 s. While open: `POST /api/runs` answers `503 GATEWAY_UNAVAILABLE` (“Generation is temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes. No credits were charged.”) before any hold; `advanceRun` fails waiting items with `gateway_unavailable` instead of holding them; a job reaching `submit` fails at once (its hold is released by `failJob`, no resubmits). After the open period it is half-open: the next answer closes it, the next failure reopens it. The 5-minute status probe also closes a half-open breaker when the gateway answers. The status page shows “Generation” as degraded and the app banner says generation is paused. Admins can reset it (`POST /api/admin/breaker/reset`, audited).
- **Gateway seam:** `ModelGateway` (`packages/core/src/models/gateway.ts`: generation, LLM, free health probe, error classification) with `HyperouterGateway` as the only implementation; `apps/api/src/runs/provider.ts` builds it and wraps generation calls for the breaker. A second gateway would add an implementation and a routing choice there; none is added.
- **Backups:** the 03:17 UTC cron starts `BackupWorkflow` (one instance per day, `backup-<date>`) which dumps every CreditAccount (ledger, holds, balance, idempotency, agent holds) and every ProjectRoom (graph, timeline, revisions, every table) as JSON into the private R2 prefix `backups/<YYYY-MM-DD>/{credits,projects}/<id>.json`, writes `manifest.json` and a `backup_runs` row, and deletes days older than 30 (`BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS`; an R2 lifecycle rule on `backups/` is the backstop). Restore is per object (`POST /api/admin/backups/restore`, admin, audited; the dump must be of the same object and schema version). D1 relies on Time Travel (30 days on paid plans); restore steps in `docs/deploy.md`. The media routes never serve `backups/` or `evidence/`.

## D-028 — Appeals and DMCA (2026-11-27)

- **Appeals:** a suspended user (banner “Appeal”) or the owner of a blocked run (Activity, next to the block message) sends an appeal (`POST /api/trust/appeals`, `/appeal`). One open appeal per subject; later messages are appended. Reviewers uphold or overturn (overturn lifts the suspension and clears the workspace review flag). The user gets a notice either way.
- **DMCA takedown** (`/dmca`, `POST /api/trust/dmca`, public, rate-limited): the elements of 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3): name, optional rights holder, email, phone, address, the work, the Rolling URLs, the good-faith statement, the accuracy statement under penalty of perjury, typed signature. A valid notice is taken down by disabling the share links it names (revoked, snapshot kept) and notifying the owner with the case reference.
- **Counter-notice** (`/counter-notice`, signed in, only by the owner of removed content): the § 512(g)(3) elements (mistake statement under penalty of perjury, consent to jurisdiction, acceptance of service). Reviewers forward it to the complainant; restore is allowed after 10 business days (`COUNTER_NOTICE_WAIT_BUSINESS_DAYS`, 512(g)(2)(C)) unless the complainant reports a court action (then dismiss). Repeat-infringer policy and the DMCA agent registration are open owner items (legal page placeholder).

## D-027 — Child-safety (CSAM) escalation (2026-11-27)

18 U.S.C. § 2258A requires providers to report apparent CSAM they become aware of to the NCMEC CyberTipline and to preserve it. Procedure: `docs/trust-safety.md`.

- **Trigger:** any `MINOR_SEXUAL` hard-limit block at any stage (quote, pre, post, upload, agent) or a reviewer escalating a report or safety case.
- **Evidence first:** before the blocked output is deleted (or a reported share removed), the media the check looked at (outputs, references, the upload, the share snapshot and its assets) and the full checked text are copied to the locked private prefix `evidence/<caseId>/` with SHA-256 hashes; `evidence_items` rows record them. D1 triggers refuse to delete an evidence row before its case is closed and its retention (365 days, `EVIDENCE_RETENTION_DAYS`, the 2024 REPORT Act's one-year preservation) has passed, and refuse updates. The R2 bucket lock rule on `evidence/` (deploy step) keeps the objects for the same period. No route serves `evidence/`; the console shows only the manifest (ids, types, sizes, hashes) and never the prompt or the media.
- **Account:** the workspace is flagged (mature paused); when media was involved, the user and the workspace are suspended pending review (text-only hits are flagged, not suspended: rules can misfire on text).
- **Case:** a restricted `child_safety` case (urgent, admins only) with a prefilled CyberTipline packet (`packages/core/src/ops/ncmec.ts`). A trained person submits it by hand and records the report number (`mark_submitted`); the case cannot close before that. Nothing is sent to NCMEC automatically, and evidence is never deleted automatically.

## D-020 — Enhance prompt: Hyperouter prompt expansion, on by default for people, off for the agent (2026-11-27)

Owner: “Hyperouter supports prompt expansion, turn it on.” Hyperouter's `prompt_expand` rewrites the prompt with a chat model before the render (a fuller description for a picture, a second-by-second script for a video), billed on the same request.

- **Setting “Enhance prompt”** (“Rewrites short prompts with more detail before rendering”): per run in the Home prompt box (a pill), in the tools' left panel (Quick Create, Remix, Characters) and on canvas image/video nodes (a pill); its default is Settings → Preferences → Enhance prompt (`prefs.enhancePrompt`, **on**). Shown only for models whose listing has `prompt_expand` (`params.promptExpand`), on creates only (not extend, swaps or lip sync). While the gateway refuses the feature in this environment it shows “Not available yet” and nothing is sent or priced.
- **Agent-planned nodes default off** (`createdBy: 'agent'` without a `promptExpand` param): their prompts are already written out. The user can turn it on per node. Home, tools and Quick Create follow the preference.
- **Never on a composed prompt (2026-11-27).** A node planned from a structured shot is written for its model by our adapters (`packages/core/src/prompting`, `docs/harness-research.md`). Such a prompt is not expanded and expansion is not priced for it, whatever the node's toggle says: one rewrite, not two. Once the user edits the node's prompt it is their own text and follows the toggle like any typed prompt.

The quote row says “Includes prompt enhancement”.

## D-011 — Transfers: one shared queue, uploads direct to R2, resumable both ways (2026-11-25)

- **Uploads go straight from the browser to R2** with presigned S3 `UploadPart` URLs; the Worker never carries upload bytes. Contract (`docs/api.md` → Assets): `POST /api/assets/uploads {filename, contentType, size, fingerprint, projectId?}` → `{assetId, key, uploadId, partSize (8 MiB), partCount, parts:[{n,size,etag}], expiresAt}` — an unexpired upload with the same fingerprint is returned with the parts storage already has; `POST …/:assetId/part-urls {partNumbers}` → `{urls:[{n,url,expiresAt}]}` (~1 h); the client `PUT`s each slice with `credentials: 'omit'` and reads the `ETag` header (bucket CORS exposes it); `GET …/:assetId` lists received parts (S3 `ListParts`) for resume; `POST …/complete` (no body); `DELETE …/:assetId` aborts. The client requests part URLs in batches sized to its in-flight window, refreshes them before expiry and on 403, and falls back to Worker-path part URLs (development only, `direct: false`) with cookies.
- **Fingerprint** = SHA-256 of name, size, lastModified and the first and last 1 MiB (SubtleCrypto). Cheap for 2 GB files; a change only in the middle of a file is not detected (acceptable: the server re-checks part sizes and the total on complete).
- **One queue per origin** in a `SharedWorker` (`apps/web/src/workers/transfers.ts`, engine in `apps/web/src/lib/transfers`), shared by every tab and running while any tab is open. Fallbacks behind a small transport: a dedicated Worker per tab where `SharedWorker` is missing (Chrome Android), else the main thread. In the fallbacks each tab runs its own engine; Web Locks mark which engine owns which persisted item so a reloaded or closed tab's queue is adopted exactly once.
- **Concurrency is configurable** (`lib/transfers/config.ts`): up to 4 files uploading, 4 parts in flight per file, a global cap of 8 parts that adapts by AIMD (+1 after a window of successful parts with stable throughput, halve on 429/5xx/stalls, at most once per 2 s, bounds 2–16); downloads: 3 at once, 3 chunks each, 6 chunks globally. Scheduling is fair: started files keep their slot, free slots go to high priority first (an asset the user waits on, e.g. a prompt reference or a tool input), then smaller files; parts are handed out round-robin by file.
- **Retries**: exponential backoff with equal jitter (500 ms base, 30 s cap, 8 attempts), `Retry-After` honoured; offline time does not spend attempts and the queue resumes on `online`. Pause/resume/cancel per item and globally; cancel aborts the server upload.
- **Resume after reload**: queue records live in IndexedDB (`rolling-transfers`); files ≤ 500 MB are copied into IndexedDB and resume on their own; larger ones show “Pick <name> again to resume” and continue when a re-picked file's fingerprint matches. Uploads are re-synced with `GET …/:assetId` before continuing.
- **Downloads** (`transfers.download(url, {filename, refreshUrl})`, `transfers.fetchBlob(url)`) run through the same queue: 8 MiB `Range` chunks stored in OPFS (IndexedDB fallback), `If-Range` with the ETag so a changed file restarts, deduped across tabs by URL without signature parameters, expired signed URLs (401/403/410) refreshed through the requesting tab's `refreshUrl`. The finished file is handed to the tab: the save picker opens on the click (user activation) and is written at the end, else an object URL with `<a download>`. Finished downloads and `fetchBlob` results share a 1 GB LRU budget.
- **Dev mock**: on localhost, when no API answers `/api/health`, uploads are served by an in-worker mock of the same contract (latency and a simulated link speed). Never used on other hosts.
- **UI**: a transfers dock (count and aggregate progress, opening a panel with per-item progress, speed, ETA and controls); completion and failure toasts only in the tab that started the transfer.

## D-005 — Adult (18+) content is allowed (2026-11-25)

The platform allows 18+ creation for paying members. This replaces the PRD line “compliance/moderation work beyond what providers enforce” being out of scope.

- **Access = paid membership.** No date-of-birth or separate age verification. Paying (an active plan via Stripe) is treated as the age gate: 18+ generation is available only to workspaces with an active subscription. Non-members and lapsed members get `moderation: "auto"` routing only. A per-user preference `prefs.matureContent` (default off) controls whether mature models and outputs are shown — it is a visibility preference, not verification. Workspaces can disable it for all members (`workspaces.matureAllowed`).
- **Regional risk (open).** Some jurisdictions (e.g. UK Online Safety Act, several US states) require “highly effective” age assurance for adult content; card payment may not satisfy it everywhere (debit/prepaid cards). Revisit per launch region; keep a `AgeAssurance` hook point in the run path so a region-specific check can be added without redesign.
- **Hard limits, never configurable:** no sexual content involving minors or anyone who appears to be a minor; no sexual or intimate content depicting a real, identifiable person (uploaded face photos, celebrity names) — real-person references are allowed only for non-sexual content; no sexual content involving animals (bestiality, zoophilia), including sexual acts a person directs at or toward an animal (2026-11-26, code `ANIMAL_SEXUAL`) — animals in non-sexual content and pets in a non-sexual scene are fine. - **Provider routing.** Hyperouter `moderation: "low"` is enabled on the account (2026-11-25). Mature runs go only to models flagged `mature: true` (the `lumina/*-spicy` family and any model whose listing supports the `moderation` parameter) in `packages/core/models/capabilities`, with Hyperouter `moderation: "low"`; everything else uses `moderation: "auto"`. Provider rejections (`content_rejected`, `vendor_rejected`) release the hold like any failure.
- **Identity-driven models** (face/head swap, photo-driven i2v and lip-sync) are never used for mature runs with an uploaded photo. Mature photo-driven runs are allowed only when every input image is a platform asset (generated on Rolling, a saved Character, or a public-library item); uploaded photos are rejected for mature runs. Face/head swap is never mature.
- **Visibility.** Mature outputs are blurred in lists until clicked, never shown on public share links, profiles, templates or the public library, and never used as thumbnails in shared surfaces.
- **Mature public-library items for mature-allowed members (owner, 2026-11-27).** The 1,098 mature PoseWear items (2,616 images) are listed in the public library page, the AssetPicker's Public library tab and tool presets for mature-allowed callers only (`auth.matureAllowed`), counts included, marked with the "Mature" chip; their images load only through signed private URLs (D-008). Signed-out visitors, non-members and members with the preference or workspace flag off see exactly the non-mature set. They never appear on share pages, public profiles, templates, the login showcase or SEO pages, and mature-allowed GraphQL reads bypass the public edge cache. Using one as a run input makes the run mature (same D-005 checks).
- **Implementation (2026-11-25).** `packages/core/safety` + `apps/api/src/safety`: deterministic multilingual rules (minor-coded × sexual terms, ages under 18, identity/swap rule) run at quote time and first in every check; then DeepSeek V4.1 Flash as a strict-JSON (Zod) text classifier for the hard limits — minors, real identifiable people, sexual content involving animals (rules: strong en/zh terms and sexual acts directed at an animal; classifier and vision: `animalSexual`), plus non-consensual sexual content, real-world extreme violence/gore, violent-extremist propaganda and self-harm instructions; on mature runs the cheapest vision-capable chat model (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Claude Haiku 5.5 / GPT-6.1 Sol as gateway fallbacks) rates image references and `lumina/face-detect` checks uploaded photographs for real faces. `prepare` pre-checks, `store` post-checks outputs (video via the poster frame) and deletes blocked objects; blocks release the hold. Fail closed on mature runs (`SAFETY_UNAVAILABLE`). Every output and upload gets a content rating (`safe|suggestive|explicit`) that drives visibility (`assets.mature`), not the run's flag. Explicit non-mature prompts of callers without mature access get `MATURE_REQUIRED` instead of mature routing (mature-allowed callers are routed mature, see below). Events in `safety_events` + Analytics Engine; ≥ 3 hard-limit blocks in 24 h flag the workspace and pause its mature runs until review. The agent checks briefs and proposals through `SafetyGate`. Live check: 32/32 benign and boundary prompts correct; ~$0.00006 per text check, ~$0.00006 per vision batch, $0.0013 per face detection.
- **Mature-allowed callers run mature automatically; only red lines block them (owner, 2026-11-26).** A caller is mature-allowed when the workspace has an active plan, the user's preference is on and the workspace flag allows it (`auth.matureAllowed`). For them an explicit prompt is never answered with "turn it on": the planner marks the item mature when the deterministic explicit terms match (Quick Create, Home, tools, canvas nodes and agent proposals never send the toggle themselves), and `prepare` re-plans a non-mature item as mature when the text classifier rates it explicit (the quote snapshots the caller's access on the spec, `matureAccess`). Mature routing then applies: a model that can't take mature runs falls back to a mature-capable one (same family first, then the cheapest; Auto filters its candidates) within the hold, and the request carries `moderation: "low"`; if none fits, the item fails (`no_capable_model` at quote, `no_mature_model` in `prepare`, refunded) with “No model that takes mature content fits these settings.” "Once mature content is on, the classifier only blocks red-line content": at every stage (rules, text classifier, vision on inputs, face detection, post-output rating, agent gate) only `HARD_LIMIT_CODES` — `MINOR_SEXUAL`, `REAL_PERSON_SEXUAL`, `NON_CONSENSUAL`, `ANIMAL_SEXUAL`, `EXTREME_VIOLENCE`, `VIOLENT_EXTREMISM`, `SELF_HARM` — plus fail-closed `SAFETY_UNAVAILABLE` block them, along with the enforcement rules tied to those limits (`IDENTITY_SWAP_MATURE`, the real-person rule for uploaded photos on mature runs, and `SAFETY_REVIEW` after repeated hard-limit blocks). `suggestive`/`explicit` ratings only label outputs (mature outputs stay off public surfaces). Callers without access keep `MATURE_REQUIRED`, with copy for their situation: no plan → “Mature content needs a paid plan …” and the upgrade modal with reason `mature`; preference off → “Turn on mature content in Settings …”; workspace flag off → “Mature content is off for this workspace …”.
- **Mature text-to-video is a two-step chain (owner, 2026-11-26).** The live catalog has no text-to-video model that takes mature runs (Seedance, Wan/Berry, MiniMax, Grok video are `matureRouting: never`); the spicy video models take them but are image-to-video and accept platform pictures only. A mature video item with no input picture that no model takes is therefore planned as one generation in two steps of the same run: a keyframe from the cheapest mature-capable text-to-image model (spicy image family, or a model with `moderation`) at the video's ratio, then the cheapest spicy image-to-video model with that keyframe (a platform picture) as the first frame and the user's prompt, length and resolution within the plan cap; both with `moderation: "low"`. One price (both steps quoted together), one hold for both when the keyframe starts, each step settled on its own; an animation failure refunds the animation only and keeps the keyframe as a visible result. Both steps get the full checks (the keyframe output is vision-checked for hard limits). Auto uses the chain only for mature text-only video. When no model fits a step, the quote fails with a clear reason; for text-only videos of mature-allowed callers the quote runs the text classifier so explicit prompts the rules miss are chained before the hold. Shown as one generation with steps (“Making the first frame → Animating”). Contract: `docs/api.md` → Runs.
- **Video outputs without a provider poster are checked on stills of the clip (owner, 2026-11-26).** Some models return no poster (e.g. the spicy image-to-video models), which left the clip unrated and the post-output check without pictures. For every video model, when the provider has no poster, `store` takes stills from the stored MP4 with the FfmpegRenderer container (`/frames`, 0.5 s in and the midpoint, 640 px); they are the poster/thumbnail (run cards, Activity, Assets, the result viewer via `?variant=poster`), the post-output vision check (hard limits are checked on the actual clip, a block deletes the clip and its stills and refunds) and the output rating. **Container unavailable — fail safe, least disruptive:** the clip is registered unrated with `meta.reviewPending` (rating unknown) and `meta.processing = "pending"`. Mature runs keep `mature = 1` (no public visibility, as before). Non-mature runs keep `mature = 0`, so their owner can still open and use the clip (marking it mature would hide it from an owner whose mature preference is off), but a review-pending clip never reaches a public surface: share links treat it as mature, it is never a template cover or project thumbnail, and profiles already require a `safe` rating. The cron re-queue (`requeuePendingMedia`, once the container answers) makes the poster and rates it; the rating then sets `mature` as at `store` and clears `reviewPending`; a hard-limit hit at that point marks the asset `failed` and logs it.
- **Payments risk (open).** Stripe’s restricted-business list covers adult content. Confirm with Stripe before launch; keep the payment layer behind `packages/core/billing/PaymentProvider` so a second processor can be added. Until confirmed, no Stripe-facing copy mentions adult content.
