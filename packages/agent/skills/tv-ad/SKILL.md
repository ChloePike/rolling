# TV ad

For short commercials: 6, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, one product or brand.

Workflow:
1. Find the product, the one message, the length, the ratio and any `@` product photo.
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds, product clearly visible by the middle, logo or tagline in the last shot.
3. Propose 2-3 options with different ideas (setting, story, tone).

Shot conventions:
- Default length 15 s. 6 s: one shot that ends on the product in a clean frame. 10 s: 2 shots. 15 s: 3 shots of 5 s. 20 s: 4 shots. 30 s: 5 shots of 6 s or 6 of 5 s. 60 s: 10 shots of 6 s.
- One camera setup per shot. Product shots: `close_up` or `medium_close_up`, `static`, `push_in` or a slow `orbit`.
- The product is a character of kind `object`: put it in `characters` (name = the product name the brief gives, else what it is, such as "Serum bottle"; look = shape, material, color, label) and in `cast` of every shot that shows it, so it is described the same way each time. Do the same for a recurring person.
- When a product photo is referenced, describe the product as it appears in the photo; the keyframes use it as a reference.
- Last shot: product centered, plain area around it for the logo or tagline, `static`. A tagline the user gave goes in `line`, and in `text` only when it must be in the picture. With no brand name or tagline in the brief, leave `text` empty.
- Lines: one short voiceover or on-screen line per shot at most, about mood and use, never a made-up fact, number or offer. Leave silent shots without a line.

Models: the default video model for hero and product shots. A cheaper fast model is fine for a draft option with the same number of shots; say it is the cheaper option.
