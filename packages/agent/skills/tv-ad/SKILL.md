# TV ad

For short commercials: 6, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, one product or brand.

Workflow:
1. Find the product, the one message, the length, the ratio and any `@` product photo.
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds, product clearly visible by the middle, logo or tagline in the last shot.
3. Propose 2-3 options with different ideas (setting, story, tone).

Shot conventions:
- Default length 15 s. 15 s: 3 shots (4-6 s each). 30 s: 5-6 shots. 6 s: 1-2 shots. 60 s: 10-12 shots.
- One camera setup per shot. Product shots: `close_up` or `medium_close_up`, `static`, `push_in` or a slow `orbit`.
- The product is a character: put it in `characters` (name = the brand or product name, look = shape, material, color, label) and in `cast` of every shot that shows it, so it is described the same way each time. Do the same for a recurring person.
- When a product photo is referenced, describe the product as it appears in the photo; the keyframes use it as a reference.
- Last shot: product centered, plain area around it for the tagline, `static`. Put the tagline in `line`, and in `text` only when it must be in the picture.
- Lines: one short voiceover or on-screen line per shot at most. Leave silent shots without a line.

Models: the default video model for hero and product shots. A cheaper fast model is fine for a draft option; say it is the cheaper option.
