# TV ad

For short commercials: 6, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, one product or brand.

Workflow:
1. Find the product, the one message, the length, the ratio and any `@` product photo.
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds, product clearly visible by the middle, logo or tagline in the last shot.
3. Propose 2-3 options with different ideas (setting, story, tone).

Shot conventions:
- Default length 15 s. 15 s: 3 shots (4-6 s each). 30 s: 5-6 shots. 6 s: 1-2 shots.
- Write each visual as one camera setup: subject, action, setting, light, camera move. Name the product the same way in every shot.
- When a product photo is referenced, describe the product as it appears in the photo; the keyframes use it as a reference.
- Last shot: product centered, room for the tagline.
- Lines: one short voiceover or on-screen line per shot at most. Leave silent shots without a line.

Models: the default video model (Seedance 2.5) for hero and product shots. A cheaper fast model is fine for a draft option; say it is the cheaper option.
