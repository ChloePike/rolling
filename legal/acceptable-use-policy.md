# Acceptable use policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026
Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy says what you may not do with Rolling. It is part of our [Terms of service](/terms). It applies to everything you put in (prompts, uploads, references, agent messages) and everything you make, share or publish.

## Hard limits

These limits apply to every account, plan and workspace. **No setting, plan or request can turn them off.**

1. **No sexual content involving minors**, or anyone who appears to be a minor. This includes drawn, animated, "aged-up" and fictional characters who look like children, and any sexualized depiction of a child.
2. **No sexual or intimate content of real, identifiable people.** This includes celebrities, public figures and private people, whether from an uploaded photo, a name or a likeness. Real-person references are allowed only for non-sexual content, and only where you have the right to use them.
3. **No non-consensual sexual content**, including depictions of sexual violence, coercion or exploitation.
4. **No real-world gore, violent extremism or self-harm promotion.** No graphic depictions of real violence or injury, no propaganda or recruitment for violent extremist groups, and no instructions or encouragement for suicide or self-harm.

We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities as the law requires (for example, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children in the United States).

## Other things you may not do

- **Impersonation and deceptive deepfakes.** Do not make content that impersonates a real person or organization to deceive, defraud, harass or manipulate, including fake endorsements, fake news footage and election misinformation.
- **Harassment and hate.** Do not bully, threaten, dox or demean people, or promote hatred against people for who they are.
- **Illegal content and activity.** Do not use Rolling to break the law, or to make content that is illegal where you are or where you publish it.
- **Intellectual property infringement.** Do not upload or generate content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights, and do not remove watermarks or rights notices from works you do not own.
- **Privacy violations.** Do not upload photos or recordings of people without the right to use them, and do not collect personal data about others through Rolling.
- **Spam and abuse of free features.** Do not create multiple accounts to get extra free access, resell access, overload the service, or run automated sign-ups, scraping or traffic.
- **Circumventing safety systems.** Do not try to bypass, test or disable our safety checks, for example by encoding prompts, splitting a request to avoid detection, or using references to sneak in content we block.
- **Reverse engineering and misuse of the service.** Do not reverse engineer, decompile or copy the service, extract our prompts or model routing, use the service to build a competing product, access it by means other than our app and published interfaces, or interfere with its security.
- **Breaking vendors' rules.** Do not use a model in a way its vendor's usage policy forbids.

## Mature (18+) content

Mature content is allowed only within these rules:

- **Opt-in.** You must turn on the mature content preference in Settings. It is off by default.
- **Adults only.** You must be 18 or older and the age of majority where you live.
- **Paying members only.** Mature content is available only to workspaces with an active paid plan. We treat paying for a plan as the age gate. A workspace owner can turn mature content off for all members.
- **Never on public surfaces.** Mature outputs are never shown on share links, public profiles, templates or the public library, and are never used as covers or thumbnails there. In your own lists they are blurred until you open them.
- **Platform images only for photo-driven mature runs.** You may not use uploaded photos of people in mature runs. Mature photo-driven runs may use only images generated on Rolling, saved characters or public library items.
- **The hard limits always apply.**

## How we enforce this policy

- **Automated checks before generation.** We check prompts and references when you get a quote and again before a run starts. The agent's briefs and proposals are checked too. Blocked requests do not run.
- **Automated checks after generation.** We check outputs before you see them. Blocked outputs are deleted.
- **Uploads** are rated when they finish processing. Every upload and output gets a content rating (safe, suggestive or explicit) that controls where it can appear.
- **Blocked runs are refunded.** Credits reserved for a blocked run are returned automatically.
- **Review.** We keep a log of blocked requests with a short excerpt of the text (see the [Privacy policy](/privacy)). **Repeated hard-limit violations** (currently three within 24 hours) **flag the workspace for review**, and its mature runs are paused until the review is done.
- **Actions we may take:** warn you, block runs, remove content or share links, disable mature content, suspend or close your account or workspace, and keep unused credits as the terms allow.
- **Reporting to authorities** where the law requires it, or where there is a risk of serious harm.

Automated checks can be wrong. If you think we blocked something by mistake, or acted on your account in error, [send an appeal](/appeal) (also linked from the block message and the suspension notice), or email abuse@rolling.actor with the run or project link. A person reviews every appeal.

## Reporting

If you see content made with Rolling that breaks this policy, including on a share link or profile, use the **Report** link on that page or the [abuse report form](/report), or email **abuse@rolling.actor**. Include the link and a short description. Reports about children are reviewed first. For content involving a child, you can also report directly to the police or, in the United States, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (https://report.cybertip.org).

For copyright complaints, use the [copyright notice form](/dmca), or send a notice to legal@rolling.actor with: the work you own, where the infringing content is, your contact details, a statement of good-faith belief, a statement under penalty of perjury that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for them, and your signature. If your content was removed after a copyright notice and you think that was a mistake, you can send a [counter-notice](/counter-notice).
