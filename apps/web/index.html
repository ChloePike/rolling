Rolling

Rolling is a video studio for creators and small brand teams, in your browser. Describe the video you want and an agent turns your brief into a plan of shots on a canvas. You review the plan, AI models generate the images, video and voice, and the editor assembles the cut for export.

  • Agent: plans shots and proposes edits; nothing runs until you approve it.
  • Canvas: script, image, video and audio nodes you can edit and re-run.
  • Editor: a timeline for captions, video, voice and music, with export.
  • Credits: every run shows its price in credits first; failed runs are refunded automatically. Signing up is free; generating needs a paid plan.

Sign in with an email code or your Google account. We use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create your account and sign you in.
